03 / A little about me

Curious by nature. Practical by design.

I’m interested in the space where data meets the physical world — where the inputs are imperfect, the systems are complex, and the answer needs to work beyond a notebook.

I have a background in Information Science Engineering and a Master’s in Data Science from the University of Glasgow. My work has focused on applied machine learning and emissions analytics in the energy sector. I like understanding how things work, asking awkward questions of data, and building solutions that stand up to real-world use.
