03 / A little about me

Curious by nature. Practical by design.

I’m interested in the space where data meets the physical world — where the inputs are imperfect, the systems are complex, and the answer needs to work beyond a notebook.

I earned an MSc in Data Science from the University of Glasgow and a BE in Information Science (First Class with Distinction) from R.V. College of Engineering. My experience spans industrial emissions analytics, generative AI, NLP and healthcare prediction. I like understanding how things work, asking awkward questions of data, and building solutions that stand up to real-world use.
